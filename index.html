    02Quality

    Numbers your customers can feel.

    Every production batch is tested for moisture, ash, fixed carbon and burn time — the numbers that decide whether a lounge reorders.

    Why coconut shell?

    • Dense, hard shell for a slow, even burn
    • Neutral taste — no smell, smoke or sparks
    • Made from a by-product — no trees are cut
    • Natural tapioca binder, zero chemicals

    Need proof? Ask for the lab report (COA) of a recent batch — or send your own surveyor before loading.

    03Process

    From shell to shipment, in eight controlled steps.

    Each stage is checked, so every container matches the sample you approved.

    1. 01

      Shell selection

      Mature, clean coconut shells from Lampung growers and copra processors.

    2. 02

      Carbonization

      Controlled pyrolysis turns shell into high-carbon charcoal, free of contamination.

    3. 03

      Crushing & sieving

      Charcoal is milled and sieved into a fine, uniform powder.

    4. 04

      Mixing

      Blended with natural tapioca starch and water — nothing else.

    5. 05

      Pressing

      Hydraulic presses form dense cubes, flats or hexagonal tubes to exact size.

    6. 06

      Oven drying

      Slow drying takes moisture below spec for clean, crack-free ignition.

    7. 07

      Lab & burn test

      Every batch is tested for moisture, ash, fixed carbon and burn time.

    8. 08

      Packing & loading

      Sealed inner bags, export cartons and a photo report of every container.

    07About us

    Rooted in Lampung. Built for export.

    Lampung sits at the southern tip of Sumatra, where coconut palms line the coast from the Sunda Strait to the Indian Ocean. The shells left over from copra and coconut processing are our raw material — a renewable by-product, not a single tree cut.

    We turn them into charcoal made for professional buyers: consistent sizes, honest weights, clear documents and a team that answers in your time zone.
